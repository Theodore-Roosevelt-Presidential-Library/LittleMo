## Narrative facts

- Arrival September 1883 (TRC dates it September 8). Elkhorn about 35 miles north of Medora (NPS), house on the west bank facing east (Brooks & Mattison 1958; NPS).
- Boat thieves: boat stolen March 24, 1886; pursuit began March 30; capture April 1 (TR diary, via Hagedorn). Held up by the ice jam; overland to Dickinson; sheriff took the prisoners April 11 or 12. The page says "mid-April." To settle the date, check the dateline of TR's letter to Corinne, TRC o286312.
- Lang's family ranch was south of Medora; breakup early to mid-March 1887.
- State Scenic River: Little Missouri State Scenic River Act, 1975, N.D. Century Code ch. 61-29. Quoted phrase "in a free-flowing natural condition" is from 61-29-02.
- Lake Sakakawea / Garrison Dam: general knowledge, no date given on the page.
- **Not used, because unverified:** river length (Wikipedia's 560 miles), origin of the name "Little Missouri," the Northern Pacific bridge date, the story that floods carried off the Elkhorn buildings (Brooks & Mattison reject it).

## Errors found in existing TRPL project files (not used here)

- `tr_badlands.md`: Maltese Cross "5 miles north" in McKenzie County (NPS: 7 miles south, Billings County); wrong founders; prisoners delivered April 2.
- Press Backgrounder §4.3: "led a posse," "hundred-mile overland march." TR wrote "two days and a night."
- Story Guide: "Kodak camera" (Kodak cameras were first sold in 1888).
- National park guide: guarded the thieves "two more days" (about ten).
- ElkhornGuide stop 8 attributes the *Hunting Trips* "My home ranch-house stands on the river brink" passage to the Elkhorn. The book's preface is signed from Chimney Butte (Maltese Cross) in May 1885, so which house it describes is uncertain. Worth a look.

## Maps

- GLO plats: georeferenced by ND DWR; the clipped copy hides the plat's title block and survey date. Look up each township on BLM GLO Records (Medora: T140N R102W; Elkhorn: T143N R102W) to get the survey year, then put the year on the layer label (currently "1880s–1900s").
- 1939 and 1957 aerials: USDA, mosaicked by ND DWR. Not orthorectified; expect tens of meters of error in the badlands.
- The Elkhorn house site marker uses the ElkhornGuide stop 8 coordinates (47.241898, -103.622276), from the NPS walking tour.

## Paddling

From the NPS Canoeing & Kayaking page: 699 cfs (2.5 ft) fair; 2.5–3.5 ft (699–1,500 cfs) good; May and June best; Medora to Long X Bridge 107.5 miles, about five days; Long X to Lost Bridge two days; Medora bridge to Cottonwood Campground 3.5 miles; Cottonwood to Elkhorn 36 miles; free backcountry permit; fences across the river; no reliable drinking water; no fires in the park. The "High water" label above 1,500 cfs is the Library's wording, not an NPS category.
